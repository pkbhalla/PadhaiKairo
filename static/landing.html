How it works
Three steps to owning your exam
No setup complexity. Just paste your lecture link and PadhaiKairo handles the rest — even when you're not studying.
1

Drop in your lecture

Paste a YouTube link or your lecture notes. PadhaiKairo extracts the full transcript and builds a grounded knowledge base specific to your course.

2

AI maps your mastery

Quiz yourself on key subtopics. PadhaiKairo builds a personal forgetting-curve model — tracking how fast each concept decays in your memory over time.

3

Coach acts before you forget

A nightly watchdog detects decaying knowledge, books a revision block in your Google Calendar, and drafts a nudge email — you approve, it sends.
