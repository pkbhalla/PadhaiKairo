Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview & Purpose

PadhaiKairo ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is an autonomous AI-powered study coaching and spaced repetition retention assistant. PadhaiKairo helps students organize syllabi, retain learned concepts using mathematical Ebbinghaus decay tracking, and schedule optimal revision blocks.

This Privacy Policy describes how PadhaiKairo collects, uses, and safeguards user data when you access or use our services.

2. Google User Data We Collect & Why

PadhaiKairo integrates with Google OAuth 2.0 to provide seamless productivity and retention features. We only request the minimum necessary permissions required for the app's functionality:

Google Scopes & Transparency

  • userinfo.profile & userinfo.email (OpenID Connect): Used solely to authenticate you, identify your learning profile, and isolate your course progress from other learners.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (Google Calendar Events): Used exclusively to create, schedule, and update personalized study sessions and revision blocks directly into your Google Calendar when retention decay falls below target thresholds.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send (Gmail Send Access): Used exclusively to dispatch proactive study reminders, flashcard digests, and spaced repetition revision summaries directly to your authenticated email address. PadhaiKairo never reads or inspects your incoming emails.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

PadhaiKairo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do not transfer user data to third parties unless necessary to provide or improve the core user-facing functionality of PadhaiKairo.
  • We do not sell Google user data to data brokers, advertising networks, or any third party.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your private data unless you have given explicit affirmative consent for debugging or security investigations.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your study data (course titles, concept mastery scores, and scheduled revision timestamps) is stored in secure, encrypted cloud databases hosted on Google Cloud. Authentication tokens are securely encrypted.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can revoke PadhaiKairo's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Security Settings. You may also contact us to request permanent deletion of your account and associated study data.
